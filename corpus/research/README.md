# Research collections

These source-linked literature collections are separate from the frozen synthetic
`../train/` and `../target/` benchmark. They are not automatically safe training
material for an existing blinded experiment.

- [Erdős–Straus, 2026-09-10](erdos-straus-2026-09-10/README.md): 13 source cards,
  selected-version bibliography, 12 explicitly inferred normalization annotations,
  claim quarantine, overlap/grounding notes, and an offline integrity validator.

Read a collection's admission instructions before ingestion. Use a separate
research problem/database; adding literature requires a new reviewed benchmark
split rather than silently changing the old one.
