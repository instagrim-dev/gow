## Source-supported account

The authors define a divisor-based function fab(n,a,b), relate admissible parameters to scaled unit-fraction decompositions, and compare Type I/II forms. They report a small-parameter search for primes below 10^14 and explicitly distinguish composite exceptions to that search window. The rendered text also studies a shifted cubic representation.

## Boundary and curator interpretation
