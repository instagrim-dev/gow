# esr-02 — Counting the number of solutions to the Erdos-Straus equation on unit fractions

Authors: Christian Elsholtz; Terence Tao.

Selected source: **arXiv:1107.1010v6**. Source date: **2015-08-02**. Retrieved: **2026-09-10**.

- [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.1010v6)
- [Selected full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1107.1010v6)

Read scope: Abstract; introduction, Theorem 1.1; §10, Proposition 1.9; submission history.

## Source-supported account

The paper separates prime-denominator solutions into Type I/II families and bounds their average multiplicity. It reports N log² N ≪ sum_{p≤N} f(p) ≪ N log² N log log N. It also classifies polynomially solvable primitive congruences. Version 6 records a correction to the statement/proof identified as Theorem 1.9 in the arXiv comments.

## Boundary and curator interpretation

An aggregate count does not establish f(p)>0 for every prime. A classification of polynomial parametrizations is not completeness of all possible proof methods. The notation f(p) denotes the number of positive solution triples, not a success probability.
