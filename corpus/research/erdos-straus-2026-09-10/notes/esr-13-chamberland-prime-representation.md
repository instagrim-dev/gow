## Source-supported account

Chamberland gives an if-and-only-if criterion for prime Type II solutions: p = qr - 4 s1 s2 with positive integer parameters, q congruent to 3 modulo 4, and s1 and s2 dividing (q+1)/4. Theorem 1 establishes that correspondence. Conjecture 2 separately asserts every prime has such a representation.

## Boundary and curator interpretation

Type II coverage is a sufficient route to the original conjecture, not an already-proved existence result for all primes. Preserve both divisibility conditions and positivity when reconstructing a witness. Do not promote the conjectured coverage statement to the status of the equivalence theorem.

## Mechanistic relationship

Connects esr-08 to an explicitly published Type II characterization. Related to the factorization and Type I/II train families, not a wholly independent proof program.

## Grounding task

Check reconstructed triples and parameter integrality on selected primes. Keep the stronger Type II coverage goal separate from unrestricted Type I-or-II existence.
