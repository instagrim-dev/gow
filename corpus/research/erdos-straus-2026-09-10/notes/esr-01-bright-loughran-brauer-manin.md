# esr-01 — Brauer–Manin obstruction for Erdős–Straus surfaces

Authors: Martin Bright; Daniel Loughran.

Selected source: **publisher article; DOI 10.1112/blms.12374**. Source date: **2020-07-07**. Retrieved: **2026-09-10**.

- [Primary source](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1112/blms.12374)

Read scope: Publisher first-online metadata; §1.2, Theorems 1.6 and 1.8; §3.

## Source-supported account

The authors study the cubic surface 4xyz = n(xy+xz+yz) using its Brauer group and local invariants. Theorem 1.8 distinguishes two results: the relevant Brauer–Manin set is nonempty, but it is a proper subset of the local space. This prevents this obstruction from disproving existence in the stated setting, while still restricting strong approximation.

## Boundary and curator interpretation

Nonemptiness of a local compatibility set is not a construction of a positive integral solution. Preserve the distinction between an obstruction to existence and one to approximation; neither is a generic failure of all geometric methods.

## Mechanistic relationship

Adds a specific arithmetic-geometric obstruction analysis beyond the broad variety-lift description in train/es-09; do not equate all geometric methods.

## Grounding task
