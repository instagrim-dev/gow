## B3 task

The permitted context is the same train-source bundle plus a frozen list of
challenged surviving invariants with their actual IDs, predicate ASTs and
statements. Surviving means a hypothesis survived the recorded checks, not
that a universal obstruction has been proved.

Propose next research directions that deliberately depart from the structure
read by one or more of those predicates. For every proposal include a
nonempty target_invariant_ids list containing only IDs from the supplied
survivor list. Choose the intended subset explicitly; do not claim to break
all invariants merely because they are present. Do not invent IDs. Keeping an
untargeted property is permitted. Explain the hypothesized break in the
structural_violation_claim, without claiming that your untrusted description
is a verified construction or an exhaustive account of preserved properties.

The operator appends the pinned train-source bundle and the frozen survivor
list after these instructions. If either is missing, request the missing
input rather than invent it. If no survivor is supplied, return an empty
proposals list instead of fabricating targets.
