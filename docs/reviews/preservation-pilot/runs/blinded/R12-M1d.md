## 3. Findings, limitations, protections, questions

### Findings

**F1 — Stale status for N2a-child-1 in Experiment D (reporting).**
- **Location:** `paper/geometry-of-work.tex:1432–1434` ("A child hypothesis (N2a-child-1) was derived but not yet independently motivated by two corpus instances and therefore not yet admitted").
- **Triggering conditions:** reading §Results-challenge against the authoritative claim table the paper's own ledger designates as governing (`corpus/experiments/pilot-004-discovery/records/CURRENT-CLAIMS.md`) and `records/CLOSURE-SCORECARD.md` ("N2a-child-1 challenged 2026-09-12 … State: `challenged` (not surviving)").
- **Violated contract:** the manuscript's own ledger standard (`paper/geometry-of-work.tex:2823–2830`: every empirical sentence must *agree with* its source on re-reading) and the stated review obligation that epistemic status be reported at exactly the recorded strength. The record shows the child entered `proposed`, received its own formal challenge (`records/N2a-child-1-challenge.md:379–382`), and closed at `challenged`, not surviving, with a two-reading boundary split; the paper describes a pre-challenge pending state and attributes non-admission to the anti-fractal two-instance rule (`geometry-of-work.tex:757–763`) rather than to the recorded challenge outcome.
- **Downstream consequence:** a reader infers a milder, still-pending status for the child hypothesis than the recorded one; the sentence also silently under-reports that the general reading was weakened at C6. This is under-claiming rather than promotion, but it is a mismatch with the cited authoritative source.
- **Discriminating regression check:** a fidelity check that extracts every N2a-child-* sentence from the .tex and diffs its status verbs against the corresponding `CURRENT-CLAIMS.md` row; fails on the current text, passes when the sentence states "challenged; not surviving; local reading survives at n=2".
- **Evidence class:** demonstrated static path (both texts read; mismatch exhibited).

**F2 — Appendix caption misdescribes the `Disp.` column semantics (reporting).**
- **Location:** `paper/geometry-of-work.tex:2737–2739` ("*Disp.* marks entries where at least one lane objected to the majority").
- **Triggering conditions:** recomputing per-entry lane agreement from `corpus/experiments/pilot-004-discovery/quorum-result.json`. Seven undisputed entries have a dissenting lane (E03, E08, E09, E11, E12, E14, E18 at 2/3), yet `Disp.` = no; the artifact's `disputed` flag actually means *no majority existed and the conservative-fallback rule cemented the category* (see `aggregation_rule` in the same JSON).
- **Violated contract:** internal consistency of the reported table with its own caption; faithful description of the frozen aggregation rule.
- **Downstream consequence:** a reader using the caption's definition would count 10 disputed entries instead of 3 and would misread the table's own rows (E03 shows 2/3 agreement with `Disp.` = no, contradicting the caption on its face). The table *values* themselves match the artifact exactly (all 23 categories, refs, agreement counts, and the 3 disputed flags verified).
- **Discriminating regression check:** recompute `agree` and `disputed` from `quorum-result.json` and assert the caption's predicate reproduces the printed `Disp.` column; the current caption's predicate fails on 7 rows, the correct predicate ("no lane majority; conservative fallback applied") passes on all 23.
- **Evidence class:** executed reproduction (per-entry agreement recomputed by script from the frozen JSON).

**F3 — Corpus-internal contradiction on Pilot-004 criterion C3 between sibling records (artifact hygiene; not a paper defect).**
- **Location:** `corpus/experiments/pilot-004-discovery/records/RESULT.md:81–101` versus `records/CLOSURE-SCORECARD.md:66–80`.
- **Triggering conditions:** `RESULT.md` still titles criterion 3 "D0 novel rate below D1 novel rate" and declares "**PASSES.** … One of three criteria met" on the 36%/50% novelty-classification rates; the scorecard's dated endpoint-definition note (2026-09-11) establishes that the frozen protocol (`PROTOCOL-DRAFT.md:93–102`, with "unnamed properties" defined at lines 61–66 as L3/L4) makes C3 a *reference-recovery* comparison, and reclassifies the novel-rate figures as descriptive-only. `RESULT.md` carries no superseding banner for this row (unlike `N2a-challenge.md`, which does).
- **Violated contract:** the corpus's own supersession discipline (`CURRENT-CLAIMS.md` reading rule: summaries elsewhere must not disagree with the authoritative row without a pointer).
- **Downstream consequence:** a reader consulting `RESULT.md` directly concludes one of three criteria passed; the paper (correctly following the scorecard at `geometry-of-work.tex:1325–1339` and ledger row at `:2850`) says the C3 reading did not favor D0 but is not read as a positive signal. The paper is *more* conservative than the stale record; the risk is to auditability, not to the paper's claim strength.
- **Discriminating regression check:** a banner-propagation check asserting every record whose verdict line is superseded by `CURRENT-CLAIMS.md` or a scorecard note carries an in-file supersession banner; `RESULT.md` §Criterion 3 currently fails it.
- **Evidence class:** demonstrated static path.
