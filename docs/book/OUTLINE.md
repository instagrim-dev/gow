# Outline — *The Shape of Trying*

*Chapter plan for the [book lane](README.md). Each chapter's authority tier is
fixed here **before** drafting. The may-not-claim column is not a disclaimer
list; it is a drafting constraint, and a chapter that violates it is defective
regardless of how well it reads.*

Tiers: **A** = draft now (core practice) · **B** = draft conditionally against
[method contract](method-contract.md) v0.1.0 · **C** = research question or
conditional argument only.

## Drafting order

Part I → Part II → Appendix C → Part IV → Part III.

Part III is drafted **last** among the substantive parts, deliberately: its
chapters are the ones whose prescriptions are still versioned, and drafting them
early is how a heuristic default becomes a universal rule by the time anyone
notices. Part IV before Part III also keeps the open questions in view while the
prescriptions are written.

## Per-chapter drafting checklist

1. Tier declared, matching this outline.
2. Contract revision named (Part III chapters).
3. Every substantive sentence tagged per
   [warrant boundaries §4](warrant-boundaries.md#4-sentence-tagging-for-chapters).
4. No sentence in the chapter's may-not-claim column.
5. Every prescription states its discretion and failure mode.
6. Every `observation` sentence agrees with its frozen artifact
   sentence-by-sentence — a path alone is insufficient.
